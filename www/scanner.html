Cell lock

          
A locked modem uses only that cell. A lock on a cell the modem cannot use (another operator, a disabled band, a cell with no real signal) leaves it searching with no internet. If that happens, press Unlock 4G and Unlock 5G: they work even while the modem is searching. The lock is kept after a restart until you unlock.
